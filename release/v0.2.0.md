# Fralo 0.2.0

A rebuilt native workbench with new compositions. Frame screenshots as a phone,
tablet, or browser window, on a transparent canvas or over a background.

## Highlights

- **Phone, tablet, and browser shells.** Safari and Chrome windows come in 16:10, 16:9,
  or 4:3, with light or dark colors independent of the App theme.
- **Backgrounds.** Solid colors, two-stop gradients, three preset images, or your own
  image, on 1:1, 4:3, 16:9, 3:4, or 9:16 canvases.
- **Composition controls.** Adjust device size, position, shadow, and opacity. Drag the
  device or pan an image background right in the preview, or nudge with ⌥ and the arrow keys.
- **JPEG and smaller PNGs.** Export JPEG with quality and fill color, or pick a PNG
  file-size preset.
- **Undo and redo** for settings and batch edits.
- **Native workbench.** System sidebar and toolbar, a floating settings panel, a Liquid
  Glass zoom capsule, and an app-wide Appearance setting.
- **Faster large batches.** Thumbnails keep up when you switch quickly through hundreds
  of images.

## Breaking changes

- Transparent padding is gone. Exports always fit the complete device.
  The CLI no longer accepts `--padding`, and saved `padding` values are ignored.
- The App offers iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max. The CLI still renders all six
  iPhone 16–18 Pro and Pro Max frames.

## Install

- **0.1.1:** choose **Fralo → 检查更新…** (Check for Updates).
- **0.1.0 or new installs:** download `Fralo-0.2.0-arm64.dmg`, check it with
  `shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS.txt`, and drag Fralo to Applications.

Requires macOS 14 or later on Apple silicon. The DMG is signed with Developer ID and
notarized by Apple. Full changes: [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/SoaringLabs/Fralo/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).
